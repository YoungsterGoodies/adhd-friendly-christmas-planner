## Frequently asked questions about the ADHD Friendly Christmas Planner

### 1. What is an ADHD friendly Christmas planner?
A set of pages where you dump every Christmas job, pick your priorities, follow a master checklist, break big tasks into small steps, plan the week, focus today, track gifts, and decide on a gift when you are stuck.

### 2. How is this different from the ADHD Christmas Card Tracker Planner?
The Card Tracker Planner covers cards only: card list, cards received, card budget, mailing schedule, addresses and postage, and handmade or bought card notes. This planner covers the whole Christmas plan: brain dump, priorities, master checklist, small steps, this week, today, gift tracker, and a gift decision guide.

### 3. What goes on the Christmas brain dump page?
Every Christmas job on your mind: gifts, food, cards, travel, calls, decorations. Write it in no order — the page is for getting it out of your head.

### 4. How does the priorities page work?
Three lists: must happen, would be nice, and not important this year. At the bottom, write your top 3 priorities, so the plan is built around what matters most.

### 5. What is on the master checklist?
Five groups with tick boxes: gifts, cards, food, travel, and home. Four jobs in each group. Tick each box as it is done.

### 6. How does the make the big task smaller page work?
Write one big task at the top, then six small steps under it, each with a done box. Start with step 1 only, tick it, then do step 2.

### 7. What goes on the this week page?
Three short lists: must do, should do, and can wait. Keep each list short, so the week stays realistic.

### 8. What goes on the Christmas focus today page?
Today's date, today's focus, top 3 today, one thing to finish today, and notes. One day, one focus.

### 9. How does the gift tracker work?
One row per gift: who, gift idea, budget, bought, wrapped, and sent. At the bottom, write the total gift budget and the total spent.

### 10. What is the stuck on a gift page for?
A quick gift decision guide: who it is for, what they like, three possible ideas, then pick one. If you are still stuck, pick the simplest idea and move on.
